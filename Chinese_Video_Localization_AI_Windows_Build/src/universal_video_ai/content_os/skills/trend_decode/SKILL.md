# Trend Decode Content Skill

## Overview
Trend Decode is a content format that analyzes and explains trending topics, viral content, or cultural phenomena in an accessible, engaging way. It breaks down complex trends into digestible insights for a general audience.

## Content Guidelines

### Format Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (vertical) or 1:1 (square)
- **Duration**: 45-90 seconds (optimal: 60 seconds)
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920 (vertical) or 1080x1080 (square)
- **File Size**: Under 4GB for upload

### Content Structure
1. **The Hook (0-5 seconds)**: Trend introduction
   - Show the trend in action
   - State what's trending and why it matters
   - Create curiosity gap

2. **The Context (5-25 seconds)**: Background information
   - Origin story of the trend
   - How it started (platform, creator, timeline)
   - Key players involved
   - Why it gained traction

3. **The Analysis (25-50 seconds)**: Deep dive
   - Cultural significance
   - Why it resonates with audience
   - Key elements that make it successful
   - Variations and adaptations

4. **The Takeaway (50-60 seconds)**: Summary and application
   - Key insights for viewers
   - How to participate or adapt
   - Future predictions
   - Call to action

### Narrative Style
- **Pacing**: Moderate, allowing for information absorption
- **Tone**: Informative, analytical, slightly conversational
- **Language**: Accessible but precise
- **Sentence Length**: Medium (12-18 words average)

### Visual Guidelines
- **Text Overlays**: Key terms, statistics, timeline markers
- **Captions**: Essential for information retention
- **Transitions**: Smooth, logical flow between sections
- **Color**: Professional, data-driven color scheme
- **Graphics**: Charts, timelines, comparison visuals

### Audio Guidelines
- **Music**: Subtle background, non-distracting
- **Voice**: Clear, authoritative but approachable
- **Sound Effects**: Minimal, for emphasis only
- **Volume**: Voice primary, music very secondary

### Topic Categories
- **Social Media Trends**: Viral challenges, memes, formats
- **Cultural Phenomena**: Fashion, slang, behaviors
- **Technology Trends**: Apps, features, digital behaviors
- **Entertainment**: Music, movies, TV shows
- **News Events**: Breaking stories, viral moments
- **Business Trends**: Marketing strategies, consumer behavior

### Best Practices
- Research thoroughly before creating
- Cite sources when possible
- Stay neutral and objective
- Use data and statistics
- Include multiple perspectives
- Update content as trends evolve
- Focus on educational value
- Make complex topics accessible

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Spreading misinformation
- Oversimplifying complex topics
- Being too judgmental
- Ignoring cultural context
- Focusing on sensationalism over substance
- Not updating outdated information
- Missing the "why" behind the trend

### Performance Metrics
- **Retention Rate**: Aim for 45%+ average view duration
- **Engagement Rate**: 6%+ like/comment/share ratio
- **Save Rate**: 8%+ save ratio (educational content)
- **Share Rate**: 4%+ share ratio

### Localization Considerations
- Adapt cultural references for target market
- Explain region-specific context
- Consider cultural sensitivities
- Localize examples and case studies
- Adjust for regional trend variations
- Use region-appropriate language and slang

### Research Framework
1. **Identify the Trend**: What is it? Where did it start?
2. **Gather Data**: Statistics, timeline, key players
3. **Analyze Impact**: Who is affected? Why does it matter?
4. **Find Patterns**: What makes this trend successful?
5. **Predict Future**: Where is this trend going?
6. **Simplify**: Make complex information accessible

### Ethical Considerations
- Verify information before sharing
- Respect privacy and consent
- Avoid harmful generalizations
- Give credit to original creators
- Be transparent about opinions vs. facts
- Consider potential negative impacts

### Trend Sources
- **Social Media**: TikTok, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube
- **News Outlets**: Trending topics, viral stories
- **Search Data**: Google Trends, search volume
- **Analytics**: Platform insights, engagement metrics
- **Community Feedback**: Comments, discussions
- **Industry Reports**: Market research, trend reports
