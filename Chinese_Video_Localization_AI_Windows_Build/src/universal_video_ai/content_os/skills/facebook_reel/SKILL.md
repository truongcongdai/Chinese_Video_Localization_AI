# Facebook Reel Content Skill

## Overview
Facebook Reels are short-form vertical videos (9:16 aspect ratio) designed for engaging, discoverable content on Facebook's platform, similar to TikTok and Instagram Reels.

## Content Guidelines

### Format Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (vertical)
- **Duration**: 15-90 seconds (optimal: 30-60 seconds)
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920 (minimum 720x1280)
- **File Size**: Under 4GB for upload

### Content Structure
1. **Hook (0-3 seconds)**: Immediate engagement
   - Eye-catching visual or statement
   - Clear value or entertainment promise
   - Avoid slow build-ups

2. **Main Content (3-60 seconds)**: Core delivery
   - Engaging storytelling or demonstration
   - Clear, focused message
   - Appropriate pacing for topic
   - Visual variety to maintain interest

3. **Call to Action (60-90 seconds)**: Engagement prompt
   - Like, comment, share request
   - Follow page reminder
   - Link to related content

### Narrative Style
- **Pacing**: Moderate to fast, depending on topic
- **Tone**: Friendly, authentic, community-focused
- **Language**: Accessible, conversational
- **Sentence Length**: Medium (10-15 words average)

### Visual Guidelines
- **Text Overlays**: Clear, readable (min 20px)
- **Captions**: Essential for accessibility and silent viewing
- **Transitions**: Smooth, professional cuts
- **Color**: Warm, inviting color palette
- **Branding**: Subtle page/logo inclusion

### Audio Guidelines
- **Music**: Trending, licensed, or original
- **Voice**: Warm, conversational, clear
- **Sound Effects**: Minimal, purposeful
- **Volume**: Balanced voice and music

### Topic Categories
- **Educational**: How-to, tips, life hacks
- **Entertainment**: Comedy, challenges, trends
- **Lifestyle**: Routines, behind-the-scenes, Q&A
- **Business**: Products, services, testimonials
- **Community**: Local events, stories, highlights

### Best Practices
- Leverage Facebook's social graph for reach
- Use Facebook's native editing tools
- Include relevant hashtags (#Reels, #FacebookReels)
- Engage with comments quickly
- Cross-post to Instagram Reels
- Use Facebook's music library
- Optimize for silent viewing (captions)
- Post during peak engagement times

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Ignoring Facebook's community standards
- Poor audio quality
- Overly promotional content
- Inconsistent branding
- Neglecting engagement
- Not using native features

### Performance Metrics
- **Retention Rate**: Aim for 35%+ average view duration
- **Engagement Rate**: 4%+ like/comment/share ratio
- **Reach**: Organic reach through algorithm
- **Share Rate**: 3%+ share ratio (social sharing focus)

### Localization Considerations
- Adapt for Facebook's global audience
- Consider cultural sensitivities
- Localize trending topics and challenges
- Adjust for regional Facebook usage patterns
- Use region-specific hashtags when relevant
- Consider language-specific communities

### Facebook-Specific Features
- **Reels Remix**: Encourage user-generated content
- **Collaborations**: Partner with other creators
- **Facebook Groups**: Share in relevant communities
- **Facebook Stories**: Cross-promote
- **Music Library**: Use licensed audio
- **Filters and Effects**: Native editing tools
