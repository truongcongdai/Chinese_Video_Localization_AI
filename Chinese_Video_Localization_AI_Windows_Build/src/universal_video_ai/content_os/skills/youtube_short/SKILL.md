# YouTube Short Content Skill

## Overview
YouTube Shorts are vertical videos (9:16 aspect ratio) under 60 seconds designed for quick, engaging content on mobile devices.

## Content Guidelines

### Format Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (vertical)
- **Duration**: 15-60 seconds (optimal: 45-50 seconds)
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920 (minimum 720x1280)
- **File Size**: Under 2.5GB for upload

### Content Structure
1. **Hook (0-3 seconds)**: Immediate attention grabber
   - Visual impact or surprising statement
   - Clear value proposition
   - Avoid slow intros

2. **Main Content (3-45 seconds)**: Core message
   - Fast-paced editing
   - Clear, concise delivery
   - Single focused topic
   - Avoid complex explanations

3. **Call to Action (45-60 seconds)**: Engagement prompt
   - Subscribe reminder
   - Comment question
   - Link to full video

### Narrative Style
- **Pacing**: Fast, energetic
- **Tone**: Casual, relatable, enthusiastic
- **Language**: Simple, accessible vocabulary
- **Sentence Length**: Short (8-12 words average)

### Visual Guidelines
- **Text Overlays**: Large, readable fonts (min 24px)
- **Captions**: Always enabled, synchronized
- **Transitions**: Quick cuts, jump cuts acceptable
- **Color**: High contrast, vibrant colors

### Audio Guidelines
- **Music**: Trending, upbeat, non-copyrighted
- **Voice**: Clear, energetic, close to microphone
- **Sound Effects**: Use sparingly for emphasis
- **Volume**: Voice should be primary, music secondary

### Topic Categories
- **Educational**: Quick tips, hacks, tutorials
- **Entertainment**: Comedy, challenges, reactions
- **Lifestyle**: Day in life, routines, habits
- **Tech**: Reviews, unboxings, comparisons
- **Food**: Recipes, cooking tips, reviews

### Best Practices
- Start with the most interesting moment
- Use trending sounds and effects
- Include relevant hashtags (#Shorts, #YouTubeShorts)
- Post consistently (3-5 per week for growth)
- Analyze analytics to optimize timing
- Engage with comments in first hour

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Slow introductions
- Poor audio quality
- Unclear messaging
- Overly complex topics
- Inconsistent posting schedule
- Ignoring platform trends

### Performance Metrics
- **Retention Rate**: Aim for 40%+ average view duration
- **Engagement Rate**: 5%+ like/comment ratio
- **Click-Through Rate**: 10%+ from feed
- **Share Rate**: 2%+ share ratio

### Localization Considerations
- Adapt cultural references for target market
- Translate idioms and colloquialisms
- Adjust pacing for language (some languages need more time)
- Consider humor differences across cultures
- Localize trending topics when possible
