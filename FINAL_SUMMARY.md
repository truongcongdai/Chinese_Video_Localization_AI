# Universal Video AI - v1.0 Final Summary

## Project Overview

A production-ready end-to-end video localization platform that automates:
- Video download from multiple platforms
- Audio extraction & speech separation
- Speech recognition (transcription)
- Multi-language translation
- Text-to-speech synthesis
- Subtitle generation
- Audio mixing & video rendering
- Credit-based user management
- Admin controls & monitoring
